Our Process

How we review AI tools.

Every tool in our directory goes through the same review process before it's listed. No exceptions — not even for tools that offer us sponsorships.

  • We sign up for the actual product and test it with real workflows
  • We check Google ratings, app store reviews and community feedback
  • We document pricing, free tier limits, API access and integrations
  • We run head-to-head comparisons with similar tools in the same category
  • We re-review tools quarterly as they release major updates
  • We remove tools that deteriorate in quality or shut down

Transparency

How we stay independent.

Some links in our directory are affiliate links. If you click a link and sign up for a tool, we may earn a small commission at no extra cost to you. This is how we keep the directory free.

  • Affiliate relationships never influence our ratings or recommendations
  • We review tools the same way whether or not they have an affiliate program
  • Tools with poor reviews stay with poor reviews regardless of commission rates
  • We clearly label featured placements that are paid promotions

If you ever feel a review doesn't match your experience with a tool, email us. We take that feedback seriously and re-test regularly.
