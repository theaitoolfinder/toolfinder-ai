How to Choose the Right AI Writing Tool

For general writing: Start with ChatGPT or Claude's free tiers. Both cover 90% of use cases without paying. For dedicated marketing copy: Jasper or Copy.ai are worth the subscription if you're producing high volumes. For editing and polish: Grammarly Premium is still the best layer to run over any AI-generated content. For SEO-focused content: Combine Writesonic or Surfer SEO's AI writer with a keyword research tool.
