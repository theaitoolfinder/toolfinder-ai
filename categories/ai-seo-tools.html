Which AI SEO Tool Should You Choose?

Just starting out: Google Search Console + Ahrefs Webmaster Tools (both free) gives you everything you need to understand your traffic and find keyword opportunities. Publishing content regularly: Add Surfer SEO at $89/month — the ROI is clear once you're producing 4+ optimized pieces per month. Running an agency or larger site: Semrush is the most comprehensive option with PPC, competitor analysis, and content tools under one roof. On WordPress: RankMath free covers the basics; upgrading to Pro unlocks AI content credits.
