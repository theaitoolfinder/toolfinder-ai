Section 5

Cookies & Tracking

We use a minimal set of cookies and browser storage mechanisms:

  • Google Analytics cookies (_ga, _gid, _gat): Used to distinguish users and throttle request rates. These cookies contain randomly generated IDs and no personally identifiable information. They expire within 2 years (_ga) or 24 hours (_gid).
  • Newsletter preference cookie: If you dismiss the newsletter banner, a small flag is stored in localStorage so we don't show it again.
  • Admin overrides (localStorage): If you use our admin interface, affiliate URL overrides are stored in your browser's localStorage only — this data never leaves your device.
  • Click history (localStorage): Tool clicks are stored locally in your browser to power the "recently viewed" feature. This data is never transmitted to our servers.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect some functionality of the Site, particularly analytics features. You can also opt out of Google Analytics tracking by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Section 7

Data Storage & Security

This website is a static HTML site hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not operate a backend database or server that stores personal user data. This by design minimises the risk of data breaches.

  • Newsletter subscriber data is stored with Brevo under their security standards
  • Analytics data is stored with Google under their security standards
  • No passwords, payment cards or sensitive personal data are collected or stored by us
  • All data transmitted between your browser and our site is encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Browser localStorage data (click history, admin overrides) never leaves your device

In the event of any security incident involving data held by our service providers, we will notify affected users as required by applicable law.
