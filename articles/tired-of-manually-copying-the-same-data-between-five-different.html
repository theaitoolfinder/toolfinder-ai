Tired of manually copying the same data between five different apps every single day? Here's the Make.com Fix Agencies and Ops Teams Are Using in 2026

July 18, 2026 7 min read By Papabenj · My AI Tools Finder

Most people believe automation is for developers with technical chops. They're wrong — and it's costing agencies and ops teams 10+ hours of manual data entry every single week.

You know the drill. A new lead comes in through your web form. You copy their details into your CRM. Then you paste the same information into your project management tool. Then again into your invoicing system. Then into your team's Slack channel. Then into your email marketing platform. Five apps. Same data. Every single time. Multiple times a day.

This isn't a minor inconvenience. For a mid-sized agency handling 30 new client inquiries per week, that's roughly 2.5 hours of pure copy-paste work. For ops teams managing internal workflows across departments, it's worse — sometimes 15+ hours weekly just shuttling information between systems that refuse to talk to each other. That's nearly half a workweek spent on tasks that create zero value for clients or your business.

The real cost isn't just time. It's the errors. When you're manually entering the same email address five times, typos happen. Projects get delayed because someone pasted the wrong phone number. Invoices go to outdated addresses because the Stripe data didn't match the CRM. And your team burns out doing work that feels, correctly, like it should have been automated a decade ago.

Here's what most agencies and ops teams are losing every week: 10-15 hours of labor (worth $300-$750 at typical agency rates), 2-3 client communication delays from data errors, and the intangible cost of your best people spending their mornings doing data entry instead of actual strategic work.

Why the Obvious Fixes Don't Work

The first instinct is to hire someone. Get a VA or junior ops person to handle all the copying and pasting. That works until you realize you're paying $2,000+ monthly for someone to do robotic work, and they're just as prone to errors as anyone else. Plus, they eventually quit because the work is mind-numbing.

Maybe you've tried using each app's native integrations. Most modern tools have some built-in connections. But they're limited — usually only connecting to the biggest platforms, and even then, only for specific triggers. Your niche project management tool doesn't talk to your custom invoicing system. Your industry-specific CRM has zero native integrations with anything.

Or perhaps you asked your developer to build custom API connections. That quote for $8,000 and three months of development time made you close that email real fast. And even if you paid it, you'd be locked into maintaining custom code every time one of those five apps updates their API.

What Changes Once This Is Solved

Imagine this: a lead fills out your intake form at 9:47 AM. By 9:48 AM, their information is already in your CRM with proper tags, a project has been created in ClickUp with their details pre-filled, your invoicing system has generated a quote, your team lead got a Slack notification with everything they need, and a personalized welcome email is in their inbox. You did nothing. You were in a client meeting.

Your ops manager stops starting their day with 90 minutes of "admin work" (which was really just copying data everywhere). Your account managers stop asking "did anyone add this to the CRM?" because it happens automatically, every time, without exception. Your error rate on client data drops to nearly zero.

Those 10-15 hours per week? They go back to billable client work, business development, or — radical thought — actual time off. The monthly value of reclaimed time for a small agency: $1,200-$3,000. For enterprise ops teams, it's often 5-10x that.

Exactly How to Use It to Solve This

Let's build the exact scenario most agencies need: automating new client intake across five apps. This example connects Typeform (intake form), HubSpot (CRM), ClickUp (project management), QuickBooks (invoicing), and Slack (team notification).

Step 1: Create your scenario and set the trigger. Log into Make.com, click "Create a new scenario," and search for your form app (in this case, Typeform). Add it as your first module and select "Watch Responses" as the trigger. Connect your Typeform account and choose which form to monitor. Make.com will now check for new submissions every 15 minutes (or instantly on higher plans).

Step 2: Add your CRM module and map the data. Click the plus icon after your Typeform module, search for HubSpot, and select "Create a Contact." You'll see fields like Email, First Name, Last Name, Company. Click each field and you'll see a menu of all available data from the Typeform response. Map "Email Address" from Typeform to "Email" in HubSpot, "Full Name" to the name field, etc. If Typeform gives you one "Name" field but HubSpot needs first/last separately, use Make.com's built-in text functions to split it.

Step 3: Connect your project management tool. Add ClickUp as the next module, choose "Create Task," and map the client details. Set the task name to something like "Onboarding: [Client Name from Typeform]." Add the client's email and project details to the task description. Set the assignee to your default project lead. This task is now created automatically with all context included.

Step 4: Generate the invoice. Add QuickBooks as your fourth module and select "Create Invoice." Map the client name, email, and whatever service package they selected in your form to the invoice line items. If your Typeform asks "Which package? Starter/Pro/Enterprise," you can use Make.com's router to create different invoice amounts based on their answer.

Step 5: Notify your team. Add Slack as the final module, choose "Create a Message," pick your channel (like #new-clients), and compose the notification. Make.com lets you build rich messages: "New client alert! [Name] just signed up for [Package]. HubSpot contact: [link], ClickUp task: [link], Invoice: [link]." Include all the relevant IDs or links from previous steps.

Step 6: Test and activate. Click "Run once" at the bottom. Make.com will pull the most recent Typeform submission and run it through your entire workflow. You'll see each module light up as it executes, and you can click on any module to see exactly what data it received and sent. Check that the HubSpot contact was created correctly, the ClickUp task has the right details, etc. When everything looks good, toggle the scenario to "ON" and it runs automatically forever.

Real example: An agency intake form with fields for Name, Email, Company, Project Type, and Budget Range. When submitted, Make.com creates a HubSpot contact tagged by project type, creates a ClickUp task in the appropriate team's workspace (different router paths for "Web Design" vs. "SEO" vs. "Paid Ads"), generates a QuickBooks estimate based on budget range, adds the contact to a Mailchimp list for that service, and posts everything to Slack. Total setup time: 45 minutes. Time saved per client: 12 minutes. After 20 clients, you're net positive on time investment.
