Running a one-person business in 2026 means competing with teams. The solopreneurs winning right now have figured out how to use AI to operate like a team of 10 — at a fraction of the cost.

We interviewed 20 solopreneurs across content creation, coaching, e-commerce and consulting to find out exactly which AI tools they use daily, how they use them, and which ones have had the biggest ROI.

1. AI Content — Replacing the Content Writer

Jasper AI
★★★★★4.7/5· 105K+ users

The solopreneurs getting the most value from Jasper use it for batch content production — creating a month's worth of social posts, emails and blog articles in a single session. The Brand Voice feature is the key differentiator: train it once on your existing content and every piece it writes sounds like you.

Pros
  • Brand Voice learns your style automatically
  • Templates for every content type
  • SEO mode with Surfer integration
  • Campaign-level content planning
  • Team collaboration features
Cons
  • Expensive for solo creators on a budget
  • Needs good prompting to shine
  • Not ideal for highly technical content

2. Automation — The Hours-Saver Most Solopreneurs Overlook

Make.com
★★★★★4.8/5· 500K+ users

Make.com is the automation tool that serious solopreneurs use. Common workflows: auto-post to social when a blog goes live, send new leads to a CRM + email sequence, pull content performance data into a weekly report. The free tier covers most basic automation needs.

Pros
  • Visual workflow builder — no code needed
  • 1,500+ app integrations
  • Free tier: 1,000 operations/month
  • More powerful than Zapier for complex flows
  • AI modules built in for smart automation
Cons
  • Learning curve for complex scenarios
  • Interface can feel busy
  • Some apps need workarounds
Free / $9/mo Core

3. Canva AI — The Design Team in an App

Canva AI
★★★★★4.8/5· 170M+ users

Canva Pro pays for itself if you produce any visual content. The AI features — Magic Design, Magic Write, Background Remover — replace what used to require a Photoshop subscription and design skills. Solopreneurs use it for thumbnails, social graphics, lead magnets, pitch decks and client presentations.

Pros
  • Magic Design generates entire presentations
  • Text-to-image AI for custom visuals
  • Background Remover saves hours
  • Brand Kit keeps everything consistent
  • Works on any device
Cons
  • AI quality below Midjourney for art
  • Some features Pro-only
  • Templates can look generic without customisation

4. Otter.ai — Never Write Meeting Notes Again

Otter.ai
★★★★★4.5/5· 1M+ users

Otter.ai was the most mentioned tool in our interviews. The AI Summary feature turns a 60-minute call into a 5-bullet summary with action items in seconds. The free tier (300 mins/month) is enough for most users with 3–4 calls per week.

Pros
  • Auto-transcribes Zoom, Teams, Meet calls
  • AI-generated meeting summaries
  • Free tier: 300 minutes/month
  • Action items extracted automatically
  • Searchable transcript library
Cons
  • Accuracy drops with strong accents
  • Meeting summaries sometimes miss nuance
  • Free tier limit hits quickly for heavy users
