✎
MyAI ToolsFinder Team
Apr 25, 20266 min readUpdated monthly

Grammarly has been around for over a decade and has 30 million active users. But in 2026, it faces genuine competition from AI writing assistants like Claude, ChatGPT and newer specialist tools. Is Grammarly still worth paying for?

We've used Grammarly Premium for 6 months and compared it head-to-head against free alternatives. Here's the honest verdict.

The Free Tier — Still Excellent Basics

Grammarly's free tier remains the best passive grammar checker available. It integrates into your browser and catches errors as you type — in Gmail, LinkedIn, Slack, and any website. For basic error checking, the free tier delivers genuine value at zero cost.

What you don't get free: tone detection, style improvements, vocabulary enhancements, advanced clarity suggestions, or the plagiarism checker.

Grammarly vs the Competition in 2026

The honest comparison: Grammarly's browser integration is unmatched. No other AI writing tool follows you into Gmail, LinkedIn, Slack, and every website the way Grammarly does. ChatGPT and Claude are more powerful — but you have to copy-paste text into them manually.

For passive, always-on writing improvement: Grammarly Premium wins. For active content creation and drafting: ChatGPT or Claude wins. The ideal setup is both — Grammarly in the background, ChatGPT/Claude for generating first drafts.

Is Grammarly Premium Worth It in 2026?

✓ Our Verdict — Yes, With One Caveat

Grammarly Premium is worth $12/month if you write professionally in English. The passive suggestions, tone detection and plagiarism checker add measurable quality to everything you write. However: if you primarily use AI assistants like ChatGPT or Claude to draft content, the free tier is enough — those tools handle heavy lifting that Grammarly can't.
