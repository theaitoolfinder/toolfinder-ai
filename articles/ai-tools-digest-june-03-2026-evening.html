AI Tools Digest — June 03, 2026 (Evening Edition)

June 03, 2026 6 min read MyAI ToolsFinder

Three stories today that collectively tell you where AI is going for businesses: Amazon is injecting AI-generated images into search results, Meta’s WhatsApp AI agent is now charging per-token globally, and UK publishers just won the right to opt out of AI search. Each one has a direct implication for how you use AI tools in your work.

What’s Happening in AI Right Now

  • Amazon will show AI product images when you search for some reason — Amazon is using AI to generate product images that match your search query, even when no real product photo matches exactly. For e-commerce sellers, this means your product competes with AI-generated imagery. For marketers, it’s another signal that AI image generation is entering mainstream commerce at scale.
  • These two founders left Goldman and Meta to build voice AI for markets everyone else overlooked — The startup built voice AI infrastructure specifically for Africa and the Middle East — handling over 17,000 calls per day. A reminder that the most significant AI growth in the next 5 years is happening in markets Western labs have largely ignored.
  • Publishers will be able to opt out of AI Search, thanks to new regulation — UK regulators are requiring Google to offer an opt-out from AI Overviews in Search. For content creators: if AI search is summarizing your content without driving traffic, you’ll soon have a formal mechanism to exclude your site. Tradeoff: less AI visibility, potentially more direct clicks.
  • Meta’s AI agent for WhatsApp Business is now available globally — WhatsApp’s AI business agent handles FAQs, appointment booking, and lead qualification — now live globally with per-token pricing. For small businesses with heavy WhatsApp customer contact, this replaces expensive chatbot SaaS subscriptions.
  • Coralogix raises $200M on bet that someone needs to watch the AI agents — As businesses deploy AI agents, they need observability tools to see what those agents are actually doing. The $200M raise signals that "AI agent monitoring" is becoming its own product category.

The WhatsApp AI Agent: Is It Right for Your Business?

If you run a business with significant customer contact over WhatsApp, Meta’s AI agent is worth testing immediately. It handles FAQs, collects information, and routes complex queries to humans — all without a monthly SaaS fee, paying per conversation token instead.

Realistic use cases: appointment scheduling, order status checks, product FAQs, lead qualification. Set it up for the repetitive 60% of your WhatsApp conversations and route the rest to a human. The per-token cost model means low commitment and easy scale-down if the volume doesn’t justify it.

The AI Search Opt-Out: What to Do Now

The UK AI search opt-out ruling will likely influence global policy. Before committing to either staying in or opting out of AI Overviews, measure your current AI-attributed traffic in Google Search Console. If AI Overviews are appearing for your key terms and you see "zero-click" traffic, opting out may increase click-through rates. If AI Overviews are driving qualified readers to your site, staying in may be worth it.

Tool Spotlight: AdCreative.ai for E-commerce

AdCreative.ai ($29–$149/month) generates performance-focused ad creatives using AI trained on millions of converting ads. Given Amazon’s move toward AI product imagery, having AI-generated ad creative that matches what consumers increasingly expect to see is becoming standard practice. The platform integrates with Google Ads, Facebook Ads, and Shopify directly.
